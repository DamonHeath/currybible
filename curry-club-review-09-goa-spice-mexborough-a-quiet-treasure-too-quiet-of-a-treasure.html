Curry Club Review #09 - Goa Spice, Mexborough. A quiet treasure, too quiet of a treasure.

Goa Spice in Mexborough falls firmly into that last category. From the outside, Goa Spice makes a proper first impression. The building looks smart, the entrance has character, and the restaurant carries itself with confidence before you have even stepped through the door.
