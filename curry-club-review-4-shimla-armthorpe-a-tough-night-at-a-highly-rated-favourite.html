Curry Club Review #04 — Shimla, Armthorpe: A Tough Night at a Highly-Rated Favourite

Curry Bible review archive

📍 Overview Our fourth Curry Club outing took us to Shimla, located at 10a Church Street, Armthorpe, Doncaster, DN3 3AE — a restaurant with an excellent reputation, multiple awards, and an impressive list of glowing reviews. In theory, this should have been one of the highlights of our journey so far. Unfortunately… that wasn’t the case. While Shimla is widely recognised for quality and is clearly capable of fantastic service, our visit simply didn’t reflect that reputation.
