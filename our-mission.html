Our Mission

The mission remains simple: review curry houses properly, honestly and with enough personality that people actually want to read it.

Honest Scores

No inflated scores to be nice. No punishment for things outside the restaurant’s control.

Group Verdicts

Individual scores matter. They give the reviews weight and stop one opinion dominating the night.

Useful Chaos

The site can be funny and still be useful. That is the whole advantage.

What’s the point of this?
Curry Bible exists to review curry houses properly: no fake praise, no lazy ratings, no nonsense. Just honest curry nights, proper scores, jokes, rankings, and the search for the best curry houses around.
