# Curry Bible Trust, Sponsored Content and Affiliate Policy

This document explains the commercial rules that should protect Curry Bible as the site becomes more profitable.

## The non-negotiable rule

Curry Bible must never sell review scores.

Paid visibility is acceptable. Paid praise is not.

## Allowed commercial products

The following are safe because they do not alter review scores:

- Featured restaurant directory listings
- Sponsored menu spotlights
- Restaurant owner interviews
- Clearly labelled homepage sponsor slots
- Website/menu-page help for restaurants
- Affiliate product pages
- Email newsletter sponsorships, if clearly labelled

## Not allowed

The following would damage the brand:

- Taking money for a better review score
- Removing criticism because a restaurant paid
- Hiding that a feature was sponsored
- Pretending an affiliate recommendation is purely editorial when commission is involved
- Letting commercial relationships affect scoring

## Sponsored content wording

Recommended wording for live site:

> Curry Bible may offer sponsored features, featured listings, and restaurant profile placements. Sponsored content is clearly labelled. Sponsored placements do not influence Curry Bible review scores.

## Affiliate disclosure

Recommended wording for live site:

> Some links on Curry Bible may be affiliate links. If you buy through them, Curry Bible may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products that fit the Curry Bible audience.

## Review independence wording

Recommended wording for the For Curry Houses page:

> Curry Bible does not sell scores. Featured listings, sponsored placements, and website services do not buy better ratings. Reviews remain based on our real experience, real group scores, and honest opinion.
