The honest pitch

Curry Bible is not a faceless advert board.

We are building a review site people actually enjoy reading. That means curry houses get something more useful than a dull directory listing: personality, context, photos, judgement and an audience that wants to know where the next good curry night is.

But there is a line. You can pay to be seen. You cannot pay to be praised.

What we can offer

Simple ways to get your curry house in front of the right people.

01

Featured Listing

A cleaner, more visible listing on Curry Bible with your restaurant details, location, menu link, contact details and review connection where available.

02

Menu Spotlight

Show off a signature dish, chef special, banquet night, new menu item or offer in a way that feels like Curry Bible — not a dead advert.

03

Owner Story

A feature about the people behind the curry house: where it started, what the kitchen cares about, and why customers should give it a go.

04

Website & Menu Help

For restaurants with poor menus online, awkward mobile pages or no proper website presence. Keep it simple, readable and easy for customers to use.

The non-negotiable bit

Review scores cannot be bought.

Featured placements, owner stories and menu spotlights are commercial opportunities. Curry Bible reviews are not. If we review a restaurant, the verdict stays based on the table, the food, the service, the value, the drinks and the night itself.

That honesty is the whole point. Without it, Curry Bible becomes pointless.
